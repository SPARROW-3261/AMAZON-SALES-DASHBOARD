🛒 Amazon Sales Analysis Dashboard (February – April 2025)
1. Project Title

Amazon Sales Analysis Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze Amazon sales performance, customer behavior, product categories, payment methods, and order status from February to April 2025.

2. Short Description / Purpose

The Amazon Sales Analysis Dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of sales performance during the period February–April 2025. It enables users to track revenue, product demand, customer locations, payment preferences, and order fulfillment status through interactive visualizations.

This dashboard helps business analysts, e-commerce managers, and decision-makers monitor sales trends and identify opportunities for growth.

3. Tech Stack

The dashboard was built using the following tools and technologies:

📊 Power BI Desktop – Dashboard creation and visualization.
📂 Power Query – Data cleaning and transformation.
🧠 DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – KPI calculations and measures.
🔗 Data Modeling – Relationship management between tables.
📁 Excel/CSV Dataset – Source data for Amazon sales transactions.
🎨 Custom Dashboard Design – Interactive and visually appealing report layout.
4. Data Source

Source: Amazon Sales Dataset (Sample E-commerce Data)

Order Date

5. Features / Highlights
Business Problem

E-commerce businesses generate large volumes of sales data daily, making it difficult to monitor performance and customer purchasing behavior effectively.

Key questions include:

What is the total sales revenue?
Which product categories generate the highest sales?
Which payment methods are most preferred?
What is the order completion rate?
Which cities contribute the most customers?
Goal of the Dashboard

The dashboard aims to:

Monitor overall sales performance.
Analyze product category demand.
Track order completion and cancellation rates.
Understand customer purchasing behavior.
Identify high-performing customer locations.
Support business decision-making with data-driven insights.

7. Business Impact & Insights
📈 Sales Performance

The business generated approximately 86K in sales revenue from 714 units sold during the selected period.

🛍️ Category Demand

Electronics dominate sales, indicating strong customer demand for technology products.

💳 Customer Payment Behavior

PayPal, Credit Card, and Debit Card account for the majority of transactions, suggesting the importance of digital payment options.

📦 Order Management

A high percentage of completed orders reflects operational efficiency, while pending and cancelled orders can be monitored for process improvement.

🌍 Geographic Analysis

Houston, Miami, and Dallas represent key customer markets and may be targeted for future marketing campaigns.
